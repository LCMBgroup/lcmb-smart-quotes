Why LCMB Group

LCMB Group services the Gold Coast and Brisbane across electrical, air conditioning, solar and battery projects. Our focus is clear communication, tidy workmanship, high-quality delivery and a straightforward customer experience.

Included

What is included

  • Solar and battery installation as listed in the official ServiceM8/GHL quote.
  • Ducted air conditioning works as listed in the quoted scope.
  • Electrical connection, testing and commissioning of the quoted works.
  • Customer handover and explanation of the installed equipment.

Important

Exclusions and assumptions

  • Works not specifically listed in this quote.
  • Hidden, pre-existing, non-compliant or unsafe electrical, roof, ceiling, switchboard, metering, plumbing or structural issues discovered during the works.
  • Switchboard upgrades, circuit upgrades, mains upgrades, meter board works, network/distributor/retailer fees or approvals unless specifically listed.
  • Roof repairs, roof leaks, brittle tile issues, asbestos identification/removal, structural repairs, engineering, plastering, painting, patching, cabinetry, landscaping, concrete cutting, trenching, scaffolding or access equipment unless specifically listed.
  • For air conditioning: repairs to existing ductwork, return air issues, condensate drainage issues, drain pumps, access limitations, controller/network issues or maintenance-related faults unless specifically listed.
  • For solar and battery: shading changes, tree trimming, internet/Wi-Fi/router issues, monitoring app setup beyond standard handover, tariff changes, retailer delays, distributor/metering delays, and performance changes caused by weather, shade, consumption patterns or future site changes.
  • After-hours work, urgent attendance, permits, traffic control, additional labour/materials or variations outside the quoted scope unless approved in writing.

Warranty

10-year workmanship warranty

LCMB Group provides a 10-year workmanship warranty on LCMB Group workmanship, subject to normal conditions and the quoted scope. Product and equipment warranties are provided by the relevant manufacturer and may vary by product.

Warranty does not cover misuse, lack of maintenance, storm or accidental damage, third-party alterations, pre-existing faults, customer-supplied equipment, or issues outside the quoted works.
